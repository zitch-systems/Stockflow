DRAFT FOR OPERATOR REVIEW

Privacy information

This V2 draft describes the implementation inspected in October 2026. The service operator must confirm legal identity, data retention, hosting region, subprocessors, deletion procedures and applicable obligations before publication or an app-store release.

Information used by StockFlow

Account details identify your business users. Business records include products, stock, sales, customers, suppliers, payments recorded by staff and uploaded evidence. Some existing staff workflows can contain identity and banking information; access and retention of those fields need specific review.

How the platform uses information

Records support business operations, authentication, receipts, reporting and accountability. The web and mobile workspaces use the same Supabase backend. The V2 native workspace keeps authentication sessions in memory; the browser workspace uses tab-scoped session storage. Uploaded attachments and business records are held by the backend.

Camera access

The V2 scanner requests camera access when you start scanning a product barcode. It uses the decoded product code to search your business's inventory. Scanning is optional; you can search for a SKU manually.

Access and requests

Business roles control permitted operations. Contact your business owner about staff access and business records. Contact StockFlow support about account and privacy requests. A production policy must specify verified response, export, deletion and retention procedures.

This draft does not assert a certification, legal compliance status, backup schedule or data-residency commitment.
